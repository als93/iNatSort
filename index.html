Before you start

This tool helps you post your own identifications faster. It never suggests an identification, and it posts only when you press a key or click.

You decide every one. Each identification, quality vote and annotation must be your own decision, made while looking at that observation.

No bots or unattended automation. Do not use scripts, bots or AI agents to choose or post them. iNaturalist allows tools, including AI, to help you work out what something is, as long as you personally decide and post each one. It treats unattended machine-generated content as a suspendable offense.

You are responsible. Everything posted with your token is posted under your account, and iNaturalist's Terms of Use make you responsible for it.

Check your work. Software can go wrong in ways neither of us expects. For example, it could post a different taxon than the one you chose because of a wrong taxon ID or a lookup problem. Open Recent posts and press Check against iNaturalist, or review your identifications on iNaturalist, especially the first few after any update, and correct or withdraw anything that does not match what you chose.
